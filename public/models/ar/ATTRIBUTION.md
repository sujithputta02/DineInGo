# 3D Model Assets Attribution, Licensing & Physical Calibration

The 3D assets in this directory (`public/models/ar/`) are verified, production-ready, mobile-optimized glTF Binary (`.glb`) food models utilized for DineInGo's interactive 3D and WebAR preview experiences.

All models have been individually calibrated to defensible real-world restaurant tableware dimensions, with base surface contact planes aligned precisely to $Y_{\min} = 0.000\text{m}$ for seamless tabletop placement in native mobile AR (Android Scene Viewer, iOS Quick Look, WebXR).
